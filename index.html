About The Researcher

Modeling consequences before they happen

I'm a Nuclear Engineering student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with research interests in nuclear security, nonproliferation, and the effects of nuclear war.

My work centers on computational modeling of radiation transport, shielding, and high-altitude nuclear environments using Python and MCNP.

I've conducted research at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in nuclear security.

High-altitude nuclear explosions & space systems

EVIDENT Lab · UW–Madison · Current

ANS 2024

Perturbations to the 30B-10 Cylinder Criticality Control System

Petersen, Z., Gaylard, A., Lang, A. [ORNL], Celik, C. [ORNL], Metwally, W. [ORNL]

PDF
This study investigates modifications to the 30B-10 cylinder criticality control system (CCS) to optimize HALEU fuel transport. Using MCNP simulations, the impact of control rod geometry and radius on keff and uranium mass capacity is analyzed. Results demonstrate that strategic removal and resizing of rods can increase U-235 capacity while maintaining safety constraints.
ANS 2024

Improving Transport Efficiency of HALEU Fuel with a Modified Criticality Control System in the 30B-10

Petersen, Z., Gaylard, A., Lang, A. [ORNL], Celik, C. [ORNL], Metwally, W. [ORNL]

PDF
This work evaluates how removing criticality control rods in the 30B-10 canister affects keff and increases HALEU transport capacity. A simplified analytical model approximates reactivity changes and uranium mass gain, enabling efficient design exploration prior to detailed Monte Carlo simulations.
